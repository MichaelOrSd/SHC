July 23, 2026

AGM Update

The Church Corporation held its Annual General Meeting in North Harbour on June 26th at the Community Center. Present from the Board:

  • Mary Ann Butt
  • Ann Power
  • Mona Pearce
  • Terry Power

As this constituted a quorum, the meeting was called to order at 7:05 PM. There were approximately 12 members of the community present.

The Board gave a brief overview of operations to date. We have raised just under $100,000.00 to date. Key spending priorities had included the church purchase (including incorporating and gaining charitable status), new shingles and mini-splits for the church, major tree/brush clearing from the cemetery, excavating and levelling, as well as crushed stone for the parking lot. Operating costs run around $3,500 per year (mostly insurance and NL Power bill). There is currently approximately $13,000.00 in the bank. The board presented that it is necessary to keep two years of operating costs on hand at all times.

We also discussed the recently launched website and gained positive feedback from the members present. You can find us at nhshc.ca.

Key future initiatives were also discussed and prioritized. The board was excited to announce that replacement of the two back windows would be happening in early July. More on that in the coming weeks, including some before and after pictures.

Key projects ahead:

  • Topsoil and seeding on cemetery grounds
  • New door and step (ideally fully accessible)
  • Cemetery columbarium
  • Stained glass inserts for new and existing windows

Exact pricing on each of the above will be obtained and communicated in the coming months. In the meantime, it was unanimously agreed that topsoil and seeding for the newly cleared areas of the cemetery was our top priority. Estimated cost is $15,000–$20,000.00.

The Board also discussed the need to broaden our fundraising efforts. Much of the past funding has come from a small group of committed donors. We encourage everyone to reach out to family and friends near and far to begin contributing to our continued efforts to both restore our cemetery and keep our church in good physical condition. You can donate either by e-transfer at northharbourshc@gmail.com, or via CanadaHelps under North Harbour Sacred Heart Church. Charitable receipts will be issued.

Thanks to all who attended. Please share this note on your social media accounts, and we encourage everyone to subscribe to receive our future updates.
